**Effective Date:** November 13, 2026

**Nexor Studio** ("we," "our," or "us") built the **PDFly: PDF Converter & Editor** mobile application (`com.nexorstudio.pdfly`) as a Freemium / Ad-Supported app. This Service is provided by Nexor Studio and is intended for use as is.

This Privacy Policy informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decides to use our Service. By choosing to use our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. However, **all PDF files, documents, and images you convert, edit, or scan are processed locally on your device** unless explicitly stated otherwise. We do not upload, read, or store your personal PDF files on our servers.

The app uses third-party services (SDKs) that may collect information used to identify you, analyze app performance, serve advertisements, and manage in-app subscriptions.

### 2. Third-Party Services & SDKs Used

We use the following trusted third-party service providers, and each has its own Privacy Policy governing how they handle user data:

* **Google Play Services:** Used for core app functionality and Android system services.
  * [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Google AdMob (Advertising):** Used to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
  * [Google AdMob Privacy Policy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)
* **Google Analytics for Firebase:** Used to understand how users interact with the app (e.g., screen views, button clicks, session duration) to improve features.
  * [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
* **Firebase Crashlytics:** Used to collect crash reports, device state information, and diagnostic logs to help us identify and fix bugs quickly.
  * [Firebase Crashlytics Terms & Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/terms/crashlytics)
* **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM):** Used to send push notifications, updates, and reminders to your device. FCM uses a device registration token to deliver messages.
  * [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
* **Firebase Remote Config:** Used to dynamically update app behavior, UI layouts, and feature flags without requiring an app update.
  * [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
* **RevenueCat (In-App Purchases & Subscriptions):** Used to manage in-app purchases, premium subscriptions, and purchase history securely via Google Play Billing. RevenueCat collects anonymous app user IDs, purchase receipts, currency, and locale data.
  * [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

### 3. Log Data

Whenever you use our Service, in the case of an error or crash in the app, we collect data and information (through third-party products like Firebase Crashlytics) on your phone called **Log Data**. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

### 4. Device Permissions

To provide core PDF editing and conversion features, **PDFly: PDF Converter & Editor** may request the following permissions:
* **Storage / Files Access:** To read, convert, edit, and save PDF documents and images on your device.
* **Camera:** To scan physical documents into PDF format.
* **Notifications:** To send alerts and updates via Firebase Cloud Messaging (optional, can be disabled in device settings).

We value your trust in providing us your information, thus we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. All data transmitted between the app and third-party SDKs (Firebase, AdMob, RevenueCat) is **encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS**. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

* **Local Files:** Your edited or converted PDF files remain solely on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
* **Third-Party Analytics & Purchase Data:** Data collected by Firebase and RevenueCat is retained according to their standard retention schedules.
* **Requesting Data Deletion:** If you wish to request the deletion of any analytics or purchase history records associated with your device/user ID, please contact us at the email provided below, and we will process your request within 7–14 business days.
